# SIM and eSIM

## Activating a new SIM

Insert the SIM, restart your phone, and dial *100# to begin activation. Activation usually completes within 30 minutes. You will receive an SMS once the line is active.

## eSIM

Most modern phones support eSIM. To set one up, open the app, go to "My Line" > "Set up eSIM", and scan the QR code shown. You can hold both a physical SIM and an eSIM on dual-SIM devices.

If you switch phones, you must transfer your eSIM: open "My Line" > "Transfer eSIM" on the new device and follow the prompts. There is no charge for an eSIM transfer.

## Lost or stolen SIM

If your SIM or phone is lost or stolen, suspend your line immediately by calling 800-SUPPORT from any phone, or via the app under "My Line" > "Suspend Line". A replacement physical SIM costs 25 AED and keeps your existing number.

## PUK code

If you enter the wrong PIN three times, your SIM locks and requires a PUK code. Find your PUK in the app under "My Line" > "SIM Security". Entering the wrong PUK ten times permanently blocks the SIM, requiring a replacement.
