# Data Plans

We offer three postpaid data plans: Lite, Plus, and Unlimited.

- **Lite**: 10 GB of high-speed data per month for 40 AED. After 10 GB, speed is reduced to 512 kbps for the rest of the billing cycle.
- **Plus**: 50 GB of high-speed data per month for 90 AED. Includes free social media apps that do not count against the data allowance.
- **Unlimited**: Truly unlimited high-speed data for 150 AED per month, with no fair-usage throttling below 200 GB.

To check your remaining data allowance, dial *111# and choose option 1, or open the app and tap "Usage".

To change your plan, open the app, go to "My Plan", and select "Change Plan". Plan changes take effect at the start of your next billing cycle. Downgrades are not allowed mid-cycle.

Data add-ons can be purchased at any time: an extra 5 GB costs 20 AED and is valid for 30 days.
