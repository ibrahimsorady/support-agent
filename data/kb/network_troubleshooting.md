# Network Troubleshooting

## No signal or no data

1. Toggle Airplane Mode on for 10 seconds, then off. This forces your phone to re-register on the network.
2. Restart your phone.
3. Check that mobile data is enabled and that "International Roaming" is on if you are travelling.
4. Ensure your account is not suspended for non-payment (check "Billing" in the app).

If none of these work, the issue may be a local outage. Check the app under "Network" > "Outage Map" for reported issues in your area.

## Slow data speeds

- Confirm you have not exceeded your plan's high-speed allowance (Lite plans throttle to 512 kbps after 10 GB).
- Speeds can drop in crowded areas or indoors. Wi-Fi Calling can help indoors and is enabled under "Settings" > "Wi-Fi Calling".

## APN settings

If data does not work after inserting a SIM, set the APN manually to `internet` with no username or password. On most phones this is under Settings > Mobile Network > Access Point Names.

## Escalation

If a customer reports a persistent outage confirmed on the Outage Map lasting more than 24 hours, they may be eligible for a service credit — see the Billing and Refunds policy.
