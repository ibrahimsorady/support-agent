# Billing and Refunds

Bills are issued on the 1st of each month and cover the previous calendar month. Payment is due within 14 days. A late-payment fee of 10 AED applies after the due date.

You can view and download past bills for the last 12 months in the app under "Billing" > "Statements".

## Payment methods

We accept credit/debit cards, direct debit, and payment at any retail store. Auto-pay can be enabled under "Billing" > "Auto-Pay" and charges your saved card on the due date.

## Refunds

- **Accidental purchases** (e.g. a data add-on or Travel Pass bought by mistake) can be refunded if reported within 48 hours and the allowance has not been used. Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5 business days.
- **Billing errors**: if you believe you were charged incorrectly, raise a dispute in the app under "Billing" > "Dispute a Charge". Disputes are reviewed within 7 business days.
- **Service outages**: if you experienced a confirmed outage lasting more than 24 hours, you may be eligible for a prorated credit. Contact support to request it.

Refunds cannot be issued in cash. Deposits on device-financing plans are non-refundable once the device has shipped.
