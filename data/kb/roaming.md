# International Roaming

Roaming is disabled by default on new lines. To enable it, open the app, go to "Settings", and toggle "International Roaming" on. It activates within 15 minutes.

## Pay-as-you-go roaming rates

- **Europe**: 2 AED per MB of data, 1.5 AED per minute for outgoing calls, 0.5 AED per received call.
- **North America**: 3 AED per MB of data, 2 AED per minute for outgoing calls.
- **Rest of world**: 5 AED per MB of data, 3 AED per minute for outgoing calls.

## Travel passes (recommended)

A Travel Pass is much cheaper than pay-as-you-go for most trips:

- **Europe Travel Pass**: 5 GB of data and 100 minutes for 99 AED, valid for 7 days.
- **Global Travel Pass**: 3 GB of data and 60 minutes for 149 AED, valid for 7 days.

To buy a Travel Pass, open the app, go to "Roaming", and select your destination region. Passes can be bought before you travel and activate automatically when you connect to a foreign network.

If you have any active Travel Pass, pay-as-you-go rates do not apply until the pass allowance is used up.
